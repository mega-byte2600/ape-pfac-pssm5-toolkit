# APE PFAC PSSM 5 Toolkit Launch Charter

## Launch statement

This project launches a patient-first PFAC/PSSM 5 leadership toolkit for the MPH Applied Practice Experience.

The purpose is to help health system leaders turn patient and family voice into structured action that improves patient experience, strengthens patient safety, supports dignity and trust, and documents follow-through.

## Patient-first anchor

Rosie Bartel is the first acknowledgement and lived-experience anchor for this project.

Her story keeps the toolkit grounded in a simple leadership test:

Would this process be safe enough for my mother, me, or someone I love?

## Operating motto

Patients over payers, bureaucracy, internal convenience, and checkbox compliance.

## Scope

PSSM 5 Patient and Family Engagement only.

The toolkit does not belong to, route through, or share identity with any unrelated project.

## What the toolkit launches

1. A PFAC launch and assessment model.
2. A patient-first decision screen.
3. An evidence-to-action map.
4. A patient story and resources layer.
5. An open resources layer for Dartmouth Health and other researchers.
6. A leadership follow-through loop that documents what changed because patients and families spoke.
