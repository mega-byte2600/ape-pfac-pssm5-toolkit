Define what the PFAC can influence before asking patients and families to participate.

Decision-rights test

Do not invite participation into a black box.

Before a substantive topic reaches the PFAC, leaders should be able to state who owns the decision, what influence patient and family input can have, how disposition will be documented, and how the result will be reported back.

Who owns the decision?
What can patient and family input change?
When must the PFAC see the issue?
How is leadership disposition documented?
How and when is the result reported back?

Boundary

Governance design is not evidence of adoption.

This charter starter is a reusable implementation tool. Local roles, authorities, compensation practices, committee relationships, escalation pathways, and reporting requirements must be validated by the host health system before use.
