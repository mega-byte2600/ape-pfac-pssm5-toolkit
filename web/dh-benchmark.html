Honest status

This is not a scored evaluation of Dartmouth Health.

The framework is real as a structured assessment method. It is not yet real as a scored benchmark based on complete Dartmouth Health data.

Scoring should occur only after reviewing public materials, project documents, stakeholder input, and available performance data. Until then, the benchmark should be read as a disciplined evidence collection plan that prevents overclaiming.

Dartmouth Health existing asset

ConnectShareCare is a benchmark input, not proof of complete maturity.

ConnectShareCare is useful evidence that Dartmouth Health has supported a patient and care partner engagement space. The benchmark should not stop there. The stronger APE question is whether engagement assets connect to PFAC agendas, safety review, experience redesign, leadership decisions, measurement, and visible follow-through.

Benchmark assessment framework

Domains for evidence collection before scoring.

DomainAssessment questionEvidence to collectAPE value-add
Engagement infrastructureWhat formal and informal structures capture patient and care partner voice?PFACs, ConnectShareCare, story programs, collaboratives, support groups, advisory roles.Map existing assets into one usable engagement ecosystem.
PFAC authorityDoes patient and family input reach people who can change policy, workflow, environment, safety practice, or communication?Charters, agenda pathways, executive sponsorship, decision logs, escalation processes.Define the governance bridge from PFAC voice to leadership action.
PSSM 5 alignmentDoes the system have a clear structure for patient and family engagement, including PFAC input on safety?PSSM 5 documentation, safety committee connections, meeting evidence, patient safety topics.Create traceability between PFAC practice and PSSM 5 readiness.
Learning health system connectionDoes patient voice feed into learning, redesign, measurement, and improvement?Closed-loop reports, project charters, change logs, learning collaborative outputs.Turn listening into a repeatable evidence-to-action workflow.
Measurement and feedbackCan DH show what was heard, what changed, who owned the fix, and whether the change improved experience or safety?Metric dashboards, CAHPS/Press Ganey measures, action owners, before-and-after summaries.Connect PFAC work to meaningful patient experience and safety signals.
Equity and representationAre patient and family voices representative of the communities served?Recruitment strategy, language access, rural access, age, disability, digital access, caregiver diversity.Make representation a design requirement, not an afterthought.
Spread and sustainabilityCan engagement practices scale across hospitals, ambulatory sites, service lines, and collaboratives?Reusable templates, training, onboarding, operating cadence, leadership reporting.Package the method as a toolkit that can be reused across DH settings.

Continuous improvement question

Can DH prove that patient voice changed the system?

The benchmark should help DH move beyond whether engagement activities exist. The stronger question is whether patient and family voice changes decisions, improves experience, prevents harm, strengthens trust, and teaches the system how to improve.
