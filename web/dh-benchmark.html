Benchmarking direction

The assessment benchmarks Dartmouth Health, not the other way around.

The APE should add value to Dartmouth Health by asking a direct operational question: how well does DH convert patient and family voice into PFAC governance, safety input, care experience redesign, equity work, and documented organizational learning?

This assessment does not rank programs by appearance or reputation. It examines whether engagement is structured, representative, connected to leadership, tied to safety and experience priorities, and converted into visible follow-through.

Dartmouth Health existing asset

ConnectShareCare is a benchmark input for the DH assessment.

ConnectShareCare publicly describes itself as a Dartmouth Health-supported, secure, moderated space for patients and care partners to connect, share information, and receive emotional support. Dartmouth Health has also described it as part of the Promise Partnership Coproduction Learning Health System.

The APE value-add is to ask how engagement assets like this can connect to PFAC agendas, safety review, experience redesign, leadership decisions, and measurable follow-through.

Continuous improvement question

Can DH prove that patient voice changed the system?

The benchmark should help DH move beyond whether engagement activities exist. The stronger question is whether patient and family voice changes decisions, improves experience, prevents harm, strengthens trust, and teaches the system how to improve.
