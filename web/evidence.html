Project synthesis

Agreement to evidence to leadership product.

My APE synthesizes PSSM 5, PFAC evidence, and my APE Agreement into a practical leadership product. The Agreement defines my value-add as a methodology-agnostic playbook and toolkit for building PFACs across health systems. It identifies two site deliverables: an environmental scan with annotated bibliography and a how-to guide for launching and leading PFACs and PSSM work.

AHRQ’s PSSM Domain 5 centers patient and family engagement, including PFAC input on safety. PFAC evidence supports this direction, but it also shows that councils must be operationalized through leadership deliberation, recruitment strategy, measurement, and documented action.

The project goal is therefore not simply having a PFAC. It is building a leadership-ready deployment model for co-production and co-design. Coproduction literature supports the idea that health care service is created through relationships and activities between professionals and the people they serve. Quality 3.0 framing reinforces the move from assurance and compliance toward service value and co-produced health.

The Chick-fil-A versus McDonald’s thesis makes the evidence practical. A health system can complete the transaction and still leave patients feeling rushed, confused, or unseen. The better standard is a learning health system where patient voice is continuously captured, interpreted, fed forward, and used to redesign care.

That makes PFACs a leadership structure for better experience, safer systems, equity, accountability, and organizational learning. This supports my selected APE competencies by translating evidence into practice, assessing patient and family needs, applying leadership principles, integrating multiple perspectives, and comparing engagement approaches by quality, safety, equity, and value.

Launch charter

What this toolkit launches.

Purpose

Launch a patient-first PFAC operating model.

Use PFACs to convert patient and family voice into documented leadership action tied to safety, experience, dignity, trust, equity, and measurable follow-through.

Focus

PSSM 5 Patient and Family Engagement.

The project stays anchored to the engagement structure most relevant to PFAC design, patient safety input, and leadership accountability.

Deliverable

Leadership toolkit and evidence map.

The product includes a playbook, evidence-to-action map, open resources, patient story anchor, competency proof, and implementation aids.
