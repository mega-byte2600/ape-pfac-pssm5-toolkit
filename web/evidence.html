Evidence-Based PFAC Summary

The core argument in one place.

This APE synthesizes the CMS Patient Safety Structural Measure, Domain 5: Patient and Family Engagement, current PFAC evidence, and the approved applied-practice deliverables into a practical leadership product.

The project does not assume that establishing a PFAC causes better outcomes. It focuses on the operating conditions leaders can assess and manage: sponsorship, governance, representation, meaningful decision involvement, measurement, action closure, and report-back.
