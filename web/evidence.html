Project summary

The APE synthesis in plain language.

This APE translates PSSM 5, PFAC evidence, patient/family engagement guidance, and patient story resources into a practical leadership toolkit. The goal is to help health systems move patient and family voice from symbolic input to operational action.

The toolkit is designed for leaders who need to launch, strengthen, or assess PFACs with enough structure to improve patient experience, support safety, document follow-through, and make engagement visible in care redesign.

Launch charter

What this toolkit launches.

Purpose

Launch a patient-first PFAC operating model.

Use PFACs to convert patient/family voice into documented leadership action tied to safety, experience, dignity, trust, equity, and measurable follow-through.

Scope

PSSM 5 only.

The project stays focused on Patient and Family Engagement and does not drift into unrelated PSSM domains or unrelated projects.

Deliverable

Leadership toolkit and evidence map.

The product includes a playbook, evidence-to-action map, open resources, patient story anchor, competency proof, and implementation prompts.
