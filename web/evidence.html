Project summary

The APE synthesis in plain language.

This APE translates PSSM 5, PFAC evidence, patient and family engagement guidance, and patient story resources into a practical leadership toolkit. The goal is to move patient and family voice from symbolic input to operational action.

The toolkit is designed for leaders who need to launch, strengthen, or assess PFACs with enough structure to improve patient experience, support safety, document follow-through, and make engagement visible in care redesign.

Launch charter

What this toolkit launches.

Purpose

Launch a patient-first PFAC operating model.

Use PFACs to convert patient and family voice into documented leadership action tied to safety, experience, dignity, trust, equity, and measurable follow-through.

Focus

PSSM 5 Patient and Family Engagement.

The project stays anchored to the engagement structure most relevant to PFAC design, patient safety input, and leadership accountability.

Deliverable

Leadership toolkit and evidence map.

The product includes a playbook, evidence-to-action map, open resources, patient story anchor, competency proof, and implementation aids.
