Quality standard

The review should end with a practical answer.

A reviewer should be able to identify the problem, verify the evidence, see the local population context, understand uncertainty, and select a tool that moves patient and family input into accountable action.

Is the evidence claim calibrated to the source?
Is the population context visible in the design?
Can a leader see the next action and how follow-through will be documented?
