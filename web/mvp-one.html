MVP 1 • Reviewer portal • Two-month build path

The first product milestone is a clean, credible reviewer portal.

MVP 1 is not the final toolkit. It is the first shareable product layer: a public site that explains the purpose, evidence, story anchor, research protocol, bibliography, and leadership logic without sending reviewers into the code repository.

AudienceDartmouth reviewers, preceptors, health system leaders, and PFAC partners.
StandardClear enough to read quickly, rigorous enough to inspect the source trail.
Next MVPTurn the portal into a stronger downloadable toolkit and implementation package.

Acceptance criteria

What MVP 1 must do well.

1

Professional homepage

The homepage must make the project purpose, patient-first thesis, and reviewer path obvious within seconds.

2

No prompt spillover

Public language must read like an intentional health system improvement product, not internal instructions.

3

Internal reviewer flow

Reviewers should move through public website pages rather than being routed to GitHub as the main experience.

4

Evidence launch page

The evidence page must explain the synthesis and point to bibliography, matrix, research plan, and source map.

5

Readable bibliography

AMA 11 references should be available as a normal web page, separated by evidence type.

6

Research protocol

The PubMed search lanes, inclusion criteria, exclusion criteria, and screening rules must be documented.

7

HAI Risk Alert

The site must include a patient-friendly HAI/MRSA page with PFAC questions for infection prevention and escalation.

8

Story and evidence separation

Rosie Bartel is the first acknowledgement and lived-experience anchor, while peer-reviewed sources remain the research evidence layer.

9

About and contact

The project lead page must be concise, professional, and easy to use with LinkedIn, email, and QR contact.

Next product milestones

Build one MVP at a time.

MVP 1 establishes the reviewer portal. MVP 2 should strengthen the downloadable toolkit. MVP 3 should add a structured PFAC maturity self-assessment. MVP 4 should add a simple action tracker or demonstration workflow.
