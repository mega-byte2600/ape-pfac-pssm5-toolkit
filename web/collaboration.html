Operating principle

Open collaboration needs review discipline.

This project shares the method, evidence trail, source logic, benchmark framework, SQL examples, and toolkit outputs. Contributions are reviewed before they become part of the public toolkit.

Workspace design

Four simple workstreams.

1

Source Intake

Public articles, PubMed results, toolkits, reports, and policy links.

2

Benchmark Notes

Dartmouth Health and external PFAC or patient engagement examples.

3

Evidence Review

Screen, accept, reject, tag, and map contributions before public use.

4

Toolkit Backlog

Convert accepted ideas into better questions, templates, measures, and pages.

Contributor standard

Simple, useful, accountable.

Contributors bring public evidence, de-identified insight, and practical improvement ideas. The project team reviews submissions before anything becomes part of the public toolkit.

Does this improve patient or care partner experience?
Does it strengthen PFAC governance or safety input?
Can a leader act on it?

Guardrail

No PHI. No confidential records. No restricted screenshots.

Use public sources, de-identified themes, implementation ideas, and collaborative learning notes only.
