PubMed strategy • Zotero workflow • Screening plan

Research protocol for a leadership-ready PFAC toolkit.

This plan keeps the evidence work reproducible. It separates peer-reviewed evidence, federal guidance, public toolkits, open data, and patient story resources so the project can be strengthened over the next two months without losing consistency.

Search questionWhat evidence supports PFACs as a structure for patient and family engagement, patient experience improvement, safety input, and co-designed care?
Primary databasePubMed, with Zotero used to organize screening decisions, annotations, and AMA 11 references.
Review productEvidence matrix, annotated bibliography, toolkit scan, and practice-ready leadership pages.

Inclusion criteria

What belongs in the formal evidence layer.

Population

Patients, families, advisors, and care teams

Studies or guidance involving patient and family engagement, patient advisors, PFACs, co-design, co-production, patient experience, or patient safety.

Setting

Hospitals, ambulatory care, and health systems

Preference for health system, academic medical center, inpatient, ambulatory, safety, quality improvement, and learning health system contexts.

Usefulness

Implementation value

Sources must support a leadership decision, PFAC operating practice, measurement logic, patient benefit, or patient risk control.

Exclusion criteria

What should not drive the toolkit.

Wrong layer

Stories are not research evidence

Patient stories can anchor urgency and meaning, but they should not be presented as peer-reviewed evidence unless the source is a formal study.

Weak fit

Generic satisfaction content

Exclude sources that discuss customer service or satisfaction without connection to safety, engagement, quality, measurement, equity, or implementation.

No action

No operational pathway

Exclude sources that cannot reasonably inform leadership action, PFAC design, patient-risk mitigation, or competency demonstration.

Screening workflow

How each source will be handled.

Step 1

Title and abstract screen

Keep sources that directly support PFAC design, patient/family engagement, experience measurement, safety input, co-design, or learning health system action.

Step 2

Evidence-to-action extraction

Extract the claim, patient benefit, risk if ignored, PFAC action, leadership action, metric connection, and AMA 11 reference.

Step 3

Zotero and bibliography control

Save accepted sources to Zotero, maintain tags by evidence lane, and refresh the public bibliography and matrix after each screening pass.
