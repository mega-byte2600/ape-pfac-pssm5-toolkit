AMA 11 • Research • Guidance • Public resources

A source layer that keeps different kinds of evidence separate.

Peer-reviewed research, implementation guidance, public data, and lived-experience resources are grouped separately so the toolkit does not treat guidance or patient stories as causal evidence.

ResearchPFAC operations, patient safety, representation, co-production, patient advisors, and learning systems.
GuidanceAHRQ, IPFCC, AMA STEPS Forward, Leapfrog, and comparable health-system resources.
Story resourcesRosie Bartel is acknowledged as a lived-experience anchor, not treated as peer-reviewed evidence.
