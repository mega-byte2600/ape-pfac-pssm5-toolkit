Two practical deliverables built for health-system use.

The APE is organized around the two approved products: an environmental scan with annotated bibliography, and a practical PFAC leadership toolkit. Supporting pages, data structures, and surveillance workflows strengthen those products but do not create extra deliverables.

Environmental scan and annotated bibliography.

Method

Evidence-to-action matrix

Deliverable 2 tools

PFAC leadership toolkit.

Governance

PFAC current-state assessment

Review executive sponsorship, authority, committee interfaces, agenda pipeline, decision rights, measurement, and report-back.

Action

Closed-loop action tracker

Capture what patients and families raised, leadership disposition, accountable owner, due date, implementation status, evidence of change, and report-back.

Equity

Representation and access check

Identify who is represented, who is missing, and barriers related to recruitment, language, disability, geography, compensation, technology, or scheduling.

Measurement

PFAC measurement plan

Separate structure, process, implementation, experience, and outcome measures so leaders can avoid attributing downstream results to PFAC activity without evidence.

Federal alignment

Domain 5 traceability

Map CMS Patient Safety Structural Measure, Domain 5: Patient and Family Engagement requirements and guidance to local evidence, accountable roles, gaps, and actions.

Decision support

Leadership review brief

Summarize current evidence, unresolved gaps, patient/family signals, decisions required, owners, and next review date in one page.

Leadership standard

Useful on the day it is opened.

A health-system leader should be able to use this product to identify the current state, see what evidence supports each design choice, identify gaps, assign ownership, choose measures, and leave with a documented next action.
