Project purpose

From patient voice to accountable action.

The APE output is a methodology-agnostic PFAC and PSSM 5 leadership toolkit. It benchmarks Dartmouth Health engagement assets, organizes the evidence base, and translates patient and care partner voice into governance, safety input, experience improvement, equity, and learning.

Evidence system

The method can be reused beyond this project.

The research surveillance workflow uses PubMed/MyNCBI alerts, Outlook triage, Zotero collections, and a structured evidence catalog. The method supports transparency because other researchers can inspect, adapt, and improve the process.

Collaboration model

Open method. Controlled workspace. Private repository.

The public site remains the source of record. The Notion collaboration space will support source intake, benchmark notes, evidence review, and toolkit improvement ideas. The GitHub repository remains outside the public reviewer flow.
