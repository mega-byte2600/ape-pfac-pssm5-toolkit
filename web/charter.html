Purpose • Scope • Operating model

This toolkit launches a patient-first PFAC operating model.

The project converts patient and family voice into documented leadership action tied to safety, experience, dignity, trust, equity, and measurable follow-through.

North starPatients over payers, bureaucracy, and internal convenience.
Patient lensWould this be safe enough for my mother before, during, and after total knee replacement?
Leadership testIf patients speak, does the system act and report back?

Charter logic

What leaders should be able to do with this product.

Launch

Stand up PFAC structure.

Define purpose, membership, sponsorship, meeting cadence, intake process, and decision rights.

Strengthen

Move beyond symbolic listening.

Connect patient and family input to action owners, timelines, metric signals, and follow-up communication.

Assess

Evaluate maturity over time.

Review representation, influence, patient safety input, equity reach, leadership response time, and documented changes.

Patient-first guardrail

Every recommendation must pass the loved-one test.

PFACs should help leaders see what the system feels like to the person receiving care. The question is not whether the organization held a meeting. The question is whether patient voice changed design, behavior, communication, safety, or follow-through.

How does this benefit the patient or family?
How could this hurt the patient or family if we get it wrong?
Would staff listen if the patient or family says something is wrong?
Would this be acceptable if the patient were me or someone I love?
