Evidence-Based PFAC Summary

A practical leadership product, not a symbolic advisory council.

My APE synthesizes PSSM 5 Patient and Family Engagement, PFAC evidence, and the APE Agreement into a practical leadership product.

The Agreement defines the value-add as a methodology-agnostic playbook and toolkit for building PFACs across health systems. The goal is not simply having a PFAC. It is building a leadership-ready deployment model for co-production, co-design, patient safety input, experience improvement, equity, accountability, and organizational learning.

The loved-one test

Every PFAC recommendation and leadership decision is screened from the patient and family point of view. The toolkit asks whether the change would help, harm, confuse, delay, disrespect, or exclude the person receiving care.
