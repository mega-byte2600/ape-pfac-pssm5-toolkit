Rosie Bartel is the lived-experience anchor for this project.

Her story keeps the toolkit focused on infection prevention, escalation, listening, PFAC authority, and whether a health system learns from harm fast enough to protect the next patient.

Patient POV

The loved-one test

Every PFAC recommendation and leadership decision is screened from the patient and family point of view. The toolkit asks whether the change would help, harm, confuse, delay, disrespect, or exclude the person receiving care.
